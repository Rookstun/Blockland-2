Speakers on the cabinet (shared by every song)

Each speaker is one synth voice on the chip. Notes go to speakers of their track's role (melody / bass / other / click); a role without speakers borrows any free one. Click speakers snap their volume and decay fast; others glide and sustain. Unused speakers are dropped at build. More speakers = bigger chip.
