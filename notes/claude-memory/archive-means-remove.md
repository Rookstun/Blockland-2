When the owner says "archive" or "mark as legacy", they mean REMOVE it from the world (ghost/delete). Never build "archived"/"LEGACY"-signed props or broken buildings to represent it.

**Why:** 2026-10-28 I built "LEGACY / Archived" warehouses and little libraries; owner was furious ("when I said archive, I said remove").
**How to apply:** take ambiguous "legacy/archive" requests as removal (town.mjs LEGACY ghosting); add only what was literally asked. See [[no-live-regens]].
